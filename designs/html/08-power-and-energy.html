B

SOLAR WINGS AND RADIATORS ON THE SPOKES

Why the spokes
With the spin axis aimed at the Sun, each spoke faces it all the time as it turns, so flat wings never need to track. The axis swings about 1° a day to follow the Sun through the year
Wing output
~350 W/m²: 30% cells in full sunlight (1,361 W/m²), less packing and heat losses
Sizing
4.5 GW peak + 0.4 GW battery recharge, +10% ageing, +10% margin ≈ 6 GW
Per spoke
~2.9 km² of wing: two wings ~90 m wide along ~16 km of spoke, ~1 GW
Mass
~1 kg/m², about 17,000 t in all. Lunar silicon cells (~20%) would need ~26 km²
No slip rings
Wings spin with the ring, so power runs straight down each spoke to a DC ring main in the base
Radiators
~3.4 GW of electricity plus ~2.6 GW of sunlight through the windows ends up as heat: ~7.3 km² of fins at 300 K
C

AIR FANS · ~250 MW

Warm air still rises toward the hub in spin gravity, so convection helps. Fans set the direction and speed so every corner gets fresh air.

Cell fan walls
In each base, pushing air up through the valley at ~0.1 m/s: ~1.1 million m³/s ring-wide at ~50 Pa → ~90 MW
Farm canopy fans
Keep air moving over the crops at 0.3–0.5 m/s → ~120 MW
Return fans and ducts
Pull air from the plenum under the window strip back down behind the homes → ~30 MW
Home ventilation
Heat-recovery units at ~10 L/s per person → ~10 MW
Thin-air note
At 57 kPa, air carries ~40% less heat per m³, so heat leaves by liquid coolant, not by blowing air around
D

BATTERIES · RIDING THROUGH EARTH'S SHADOW

Orbit
~200,000 km from Earth. Earth's shadow there is ~10,900 km wide; at 1.4 km/s the ring crosses it in ~2.1 h
Night inside
Made by closing the louvres. The wings keep making power through it, so "night" needs no batteries
During an eclipse
Shed to ~1.6 GW: farm LEDs off (plants handle a few dark hours), industry paused, air, water, homes and safety kept on
Battery size
1.6 GW × 2.5 h = 4 GWh usable → ~6 GWh installed (80% depth, ageing margin)
Mass and place
~37,500 t of LFP cells, as 30 banks of 200 MWh in the district plants, close to the loads
Also covers
A spoke's wings going offline (−1 GW) for hours while loads are shed
Pumped storage?
Lifting water rim to hub stores 85 kJ/kg; 4 GWh needs ~170,000 t at the hub. Too heavy for the spokes, so batteries win
