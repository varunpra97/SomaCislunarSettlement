THE BAY FACTORY AT WORK

B

WHAT IT'S MADE OF

It works like a tunnel-boring machine: the rear ring grips the finished tube, the front ring holds the bay being built, and the two inch forward together.

Rail rings
Two, 180 m across, each made of 32 folding box-truss segments (17.6 m long, 2.5 m deep) with a rail on the inner face
Work ring (front)
Holds the new frame in place; carries 4 rail carts with walker arms
Gripper ring (rear)
12 pads clamp the last finished frame
Telescoping beams
6, three-section, 4–22 m; they pull and push the rings 18 m per cycle
Loading arms
2, one at each docking port
Weld crawlers
6 friction-stir welders
Bulkheads
2 inflatable 150 m discs that seal the bay for the leak test
Power
~1 MW roll-out solar wings plus radiators
Hub
One Starship stays docked: control computers, spares, robot garage
Mass
≈330 t plus the hub ship, 7 Starship flights

D

HOW IT BUILDS ITSELF

1 · HUB ARRIVES

The hub Starship parks at the spoke site, unfolds its loading arms and sets out the starter rail with two carts on it.

2 · GROW THE RING

A loading arm unfolds each truss segment, a cart's arm bolts it to the rail end, then the cart drives onto it and repeats. 32 segments close the work ring.

3 · SECOND RING

The same loop builds the gripper ring, then the 6 telescoping beams join the two rings.

4 · FIT OUT

Gripper pads, solar wings, radiators, weld crawlers and the second docking port go on.

5 · START BAY 1

The first frame and bulkhead go in at the spoke junction and the factory starts building.

E

HOW IT MOVES · ONE CYCLE

a · BUILD

Rear ring grips the finished frame, beams at 22 m; the bay is built between the rings.

b · PULL

Front ring grips the new frame; rear releases and the beams shrink to 4 m, pulling it forward.

c · PUSH

Rear grips again; the beams extend 18 m and the front ring moves to the next frame position.
